Privacy Policy — Notenra

Effective date: July 6, 2026
Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Notenra (the "App") is a clinical documentation companion operated by Anot Health ("we", "us", "our"). The App is provided exclusively to licensed clinicians and authorized clinical staff of healthcare organizations that have entered into an agreement with us. It is not a consumer product and is not available for public sign-up.

In the course of providing the App, we act as a service provider / business associate to the healthcare organizations ("Covered Entities") that use it. Protected Health Information (PHI) processed through the App is owned and controlled by those organizations. Our handling of PHI is governed by a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each organization.

2. Scope

This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, stored, and protected, and the choices available to users. It applies to the Notenra mobile application for Android and iOS.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Clinician account information

  • Email address / username used to sign in
  • Display name and professional role (clinician)
  • Authentication credentials (passwords are never stored in plain text — they are verified against the server and, where cached, stored as a salted hash)
  • On-device biometric authentication (fingerprint/face) — this is processed entirely by the device operating system; biometric data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to us

3.2 Patient / clinical information (PHI)

Entered by the clinician or synced from the healthcare organization's system:

  • Patient name and medical record number (MRN)
  • Age, date of birth, and gender
  • Medical history and clinical notes
  • Visit type, visit date, and care priority
  • Emergency contact name and phone number
  • Audio recordings of clinical visits, captured with consent, for the purpose of generating clinical documentation

3.3 Operational / security information

  • An audit log of security-relevant actions within the App (e.g., sign-in, recording created, record accessed), including the acting clinician, timestamp, and the affected patient record identifier
  • Upload status of recordings (pending / uploaded / failed) to support reliable, offline-tolerant syncing

3.4 What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no advertising
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or files beyond the audio the clinician records in-app
  • No data collected from or about patients as app "users" — patients are not users of the App

4. Microphone and permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, each for a specific purpose:

PermissionWhy it is used
MicrophoneTo record clinical visit audio, only after the clinician starts a recording and patient consent is confirmed. A foreground service keeps the recording reliable if the screen locks; recording never starts on its own.
BiometricTo unlock the app's secure vault using the device's fingerprint/face. Handled by the OS; no biometric data is transmitted.
NotificationsTo show status messages (e.g., "recording uploaded", "a note is ready"). Notifications contain no patient-identifying information.
Internet / network stateTo securely sync recordings and clinical data with the backend and to detect connectivity for offline-tolerant uploads.

5. How we use information

  • To authenticate clinicians and enforce clinician-only access
  • To record clinical visits and generate clinical documentation for the treating clinician
  • To sync clinical data securely with the healthcare organization's backend
  • To maintain a security audit trail as required for healthcare compliance
  • To provide app functionality such as offline recording and reliable upload

We do not sell personal or health information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

6. How information is shared

Information is transmitted only to the healthcare organization's secure Notenra backend for processing (secure storage and generation of clinical documentation). We do not share personal or health information with any third party for that party's own purposes. We may disclose information only: (a) to the Covered Entity that owns the data; (b) to service providers acting under a Business Associate Agreement solely to operate the service; or (c) where required by law.

7. Data security

  • Encryption in transit: all network communication uses TLS/HTTPS. Cleartext traffic is disabled.
  • Encryption at rest: the on-device database is encrypted (SQLCipher) and audio files are encrypted with AES-GCM in a secured vault. Encryption keys are held in the device's hardware-backed secure storage / keystore.
  • Access controls: clinician-only sign-in, biometric-gated vault, and automatic sign-out after a period of inactivity.
  • Screen protection: the App marks its windows as secure to prevent screenshots and screen recording of PHI.
  • No device backups of PHI: app data is excluded from OS cloud backups.
  • Data minimization: recordings are purged from the device after a limited retention window once uploaded.

8. Data retention and deletion

Clinical data and recordings are retained on the device only as long as needed to complete their purpose (for example, recordings are removed from the device after they are uploaded and beyond a short retention window). Server-side retention is governed by the healthcare organization's policies and our agreement with them. Because the healthcare organization is the owner of the PHI, requests to access, correct, or delete patient records should be directed to that organization. Clinicians may request deletion of their own account information by contacting us at the address below.

9. Children

The App is intended for use by professional clinicians. It is not directed to children and is not available for public download and sign-up. Any patient information (which may relate to patients of any age) is processed on behalf of, and under the control of, the healthcare organization.

10. Your choices

  • Clinicians control when recording starts and stops, and patient consent is confirmed before recording.
  • Device permissions (microphone, notifications, biometric) can be managed in the device's system settings.
  • Signing out securely purges in-memory patient data from the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through notice within the App or to the healthcare organization.
